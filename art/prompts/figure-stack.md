THIS CHARACTER: a compact, rounded triangular creature in muted olive green,
standing with its two small feet planted close together. A vertical stack of
three soft cream felt blocks balances on the flat top of its head, perfectly
aligned and decreasing slightly in width toward the top. Both thin arms reach
up to steady the lowest block, while its tiny dark dot eyes glance upward with
focused concentration. Its mouth is small and neutral. Earnest and
over-responsible, trying very hard to keep everything exactly right so nothing
will go wrong — not proud or competitive. Keep the entire stack inside the
frame. No letters, numbers, trophy, medal, books, ruler, clothing or extra
props.
