THIS CHARACTER: a small, irregular pebble-shaped creature in soft cerulean
blue, sitting comfortably with a cream sheet of paper tilted across its lap.
One short arm steadies the paper while the other uses a chunky dusty-pink
crayon to make one loose, wandering line. Its face tilts down toward the page,
with curious dark dot eyes and a subtle contented mouth. Open, playful and
absorbed in discovering what appears, with no concern for making it perfect.
Keep the paper and crayon close to the body. Only abstract marks on the paper;
no letters, recognizable symbols, easel, palette, artist costume, scattered
supplies or exaggerated grin.
