STYLE (applies to every image in this set)

A soft surrealist storybook dream, rendered like a still from a cinematic film
shot on warm 1970s film stock: photographic and painterly at once, with gentle
film grain and diffuse, smooth light. The world feels half-remembered —
familiar but quietly impossible — tender, spacious, peaceful, slightly
melancholy, always warm.

Palette: dusty pinks, soft sage and meadow greens, creamy whites, gentle
cerulean blues, with occasional small warm points of light. Surfaces are
softly glossy and hyper-real while keeping the softness of a painted world.

Composition: front-on and level, like a theatre stage set seen from the
stalls — camera at eye height, horizon fairly high, very little perspective
depth, calm and balanced. One quiet surreal idea at most; never crowded.

Never: darkness or menace, cold or harsh light, chaotic effects, text,
lettering, logos, watermarks, people, or imitation of a specific artist.
