SUBJECT: one character for the inner landscape (a "part" of a person)

A single small creature, full body, centred, standing on nothing, on a plain,
flat, evenly lit pure white studio background. Only a soft contact shadow
directly beneath it; no other shadows, props, ground or scenery.

Character style: a tactile soft sculpture — like a hand-made matte clay or
felted figure photographed in diffuse daylight. Simple, stable silhouette that
reads clearly when small. A minimal face: two small dark dot eyes, maybe a
faint blush. Strange but kind, gentle, with a trace of vulnerability. Not a
cartoon mascot, not cute-for-cute's-sake, not human, not a monster, never a
villain or a joke. Leave clear white space around the whole figure.
