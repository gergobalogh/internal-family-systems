THIS CHARACTER: a small, narrow creature in pale dusty apricot, standing
almost front-on with its shoulders curved gently inward. Both arms are
unusually long and wrap clearly around its own upper body in a full
self-embrace. The arms cross high over the chest in a soft X, with each rounded
paddle-like end visibly resting on the opposite outer side of the body. Use
subtle contact shadows between the overlapping arms so the pose remains
readable when small. Its head tilts and rests lightly against the upper
forearm, while two tiny dark bead eyes remain open and look toward the empty
space beside it. Quietly self-comforting and still hoping for connection — not
asleep, cold, frightened or helpless. No tears, broken-heart symbol, bandages,
scars, dramatic frown or extra props.
