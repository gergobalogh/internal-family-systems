SUBJECT: "How it is" — the inner landscape by day (master plate)

A wide open meadow seen straight on, like an empty stage set waiting for its
players.

- Upper ~40%: a clear, gentle cerulean sky fading to a warm creamy haze at the
  horizon, with four or five large, soft, sunlit pink cumulus clouds spread
  calmly across it.
- Along the horizon at ~40% height: a low, softly rolling line of distant hills
  with a few small round bushes. On the crest, a little right of centre, a tiny
  mint-green clapboard house with a dusty-pink roof — small, far, familiar.
- Lower ~60%: an open, softly mown meadow of fresh green grass, nearly empty
  and uncluttered (small figures will be placed on it later). At the exact
  centre of the frame, a soft, wide pool of warm sunlight rests on the grass.

Keep the centre of the image clear and the composition readable when cropped
to a tall phone frame (the middle third must work on its own).
