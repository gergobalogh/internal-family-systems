THIS CHARACTER: a medium-height, softly tapered creature in muted warm ochre,
standing on two tiny feet and holding a small cream accordion-fold map open in
both hands. It holds the map low enough that its face and most of its body stay
visible, peering just over the top edge with focused dark dot eyes. The map has
only subtle fold lines, with no writing or symbols. Careful and responsible,
trying to anticipate every turn and keep everyone from getting lost — not
bossy or rigid. No glasses, pencil, clipboard, clothing or extra props.
