THIS CHARACTER: a compact, slightly lopsided creature in muted teal, standing
with its small body angled gently to one side. It holds a short brass-and-cream
kaleidoscope to one eye with both hands, tilted slightly upward. Its other tiny
dark dot eye remains visible. The creature leans into the view, quietly
absorbed in somewhere more colorful — seeking a brief way out when the present
feels like too much, rather than simply sightseeing. Keep the kaleidoscope
close to the body and fully inside the frame. No telescope, binoculars, screen,
stars, psychedelic scenery, floating effects or extra props.
