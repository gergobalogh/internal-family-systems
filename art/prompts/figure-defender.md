THIS CHARACTER: a broad, low, shield-shaped creature in muted dusty coral,
standing firmly with two short legs planted slightly apart. Its thick,
paddle-shaped arms reach outward with open ends, forming a gentle but definite
barrier, as if saying “enough.” Tiny dark dot eyes, alert and determined, with
a neutral mouth. Protective rather than aggressive — quick to step forward
when something feels unsafe. No weapon, armour, clenched fists, angry eyebrows
or threatening expression.
